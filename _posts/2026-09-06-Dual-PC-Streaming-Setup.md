# Dual PC Streaming Setup - The easiest way possible (no capture card needed)!

A dual PC streaming setup doesn't have to start with an expensive capture card. In this video I walk through the whole process of getting your gaming and streaming machines working together — using **NDI over your local network** instead of a capture card.

## Quick note on what this covers

We go end to end, from the hardware you actually need to the software that ties it together:

- **Hardware requirements** — what both PCs need, and why you probably already have it
- **How to connect everything** — the cabling and network basics
- **NDI setup on the gaming PC** — sending the feed over your LAN
- **NDI setup on the streaming PC** — receiving it and taking over the encoding load
- **OBS Studio setup** — getting your scenes and encoder ready on the streaming side
- **OBS NDI plugin** — the piece that makes the two machines talk to each other
- **Microphone setup** — routing your mic from the streaming PC to the gaming PC
- **Final thoughts** — what I'd do differently

The result is a second PC that owns all the streaming work, so your gaming PC can focus on gaming — without spending a cent on a capture card.
